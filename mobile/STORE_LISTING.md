## Full description – English
Not sure what to cook today? What's for Dinner? suggests thousands of breakfast, lunch and dinner recipes from the UAE and the Gulf, the Levant, Egypt, Iraq, North Africa and the Mediterranean, plus India, Pakistan, the Philippines, Indonesia and more.

• Search by dish or by an ingredient you have at home.
• Change the number of people and the quantities adjust.
• Tick what you already have and send only the missing items as a WhatsApp shopping list.
• Watch how it's made on YouTube with one tap.
• Step-by-step cooking mode with big text and a timer for each step.
• Save your favourite recipes.
• Works offline, in six languages: Arabic, English, Urdu, Hindi, Filipino and Indonesian.

Made by Rashed Ahmed Alzaabi.
